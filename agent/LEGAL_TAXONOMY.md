# Legal Taxonomy & Contract Clause Reference Guide

This reference guide provides standard classification guidelines for legal clause analysis and legal document classification.

## 1. CONTRACT CLAUSE CLASSIFICATION TAXONOMY
When reviewing commercial contracts, Master Services Agreements (MSA), SLAs, or Terms of Service, evaluate the following core clauses:

- **Limitation of Liability (LoL)**: Evaluates monetary caps, exclusion of consequential/indirect damages, and exceptions (e.g. gross negligence, willful misconduct, IP infringement).
- **Indemnification & Hold Harmless**: Assesses third-party defense obligations, IP infringement indemnities, and mutual vs. unilateral coverage.
- **Intellectual Property Rights (IPR)**: Identifies background IP, work-for-hire assignment, feedback licenses, and open-source software (OSS) copyleft contamination risks.
- **Data Protection & Confidentiality (NDA)**: Reviews GDPR Data Processing Addendums (DPA), breach notification SLAs (e.g. 72-hour window), and definition of confidential information.
- **Termination & Default**: Identifies termination for convenience, termination for material breach, cure periods, and post-termination transition obligations.
- **Governing Law & Dispute Resolution**: Evaluates choice of law, venue jurisdiction, mandatory arbitration, and class action waivers.

## 2. LITIGATION & REGULATORY DISCLOSURE CATEGORIES
- **Antitrust & Competition Law**: Market power abuse, merger review, price-fixing, and exclusive dealing investigations.
- **Securities & Disclosure Litigation**: Rule 10b-5 class actions, misrepresentation of material facts, and insider trading disclosures.
- **Intellectual Property Patent & Trademark Disputes**: Patent infringement suits, ITC section 337 exclusion orders, and trademark dilution claims.
- **Employment & Labor Compliance**: Non-compete enforceability, misclassification of independent contractors, and wage/hour class actions.
- **Data Privacy & AI Regulatory Exposure**: GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, EU AI Act risk tiering (Prohibited, High Risk, Specific Transparency), and FTC Section 5 enforcement actions.

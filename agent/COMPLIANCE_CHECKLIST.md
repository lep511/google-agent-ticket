# Legal & Regulatory Compliance Audit Checklist

This audit checklist guides the legal agent during statutory compliance assessments.

## 1. GENERAL DATA PROTECTION REGULATION (GDPR) & PRIVACY AUDIT
- [ ] Lawful basis for data processing (Consent, Legitimate Interest, Contractual Necessity).
- [ ] Data Subject Access Request (DSAR) mechanism and response timelines.
- [ ] Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA) for high-risk processing operations.
- [ ] Cross-border data transfer mechanisms (EU Standard Contractual Clauses - SCCs, Data Privacy Framework).
- [ ] Security of processing & encryption standards (Article 32).

## 2. EU ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE ACT (EU AI ACT) COMPLIANCE
- [ ] System classification: Unacceptable risk (Prohibited), High-Risk AI, General Purpose AI (GPAI), or Minimal risk.
- [ ] For High-Risk AI: Risk management system, data governance, technical documentation, record-keeping, and human oversight.
- [ ] Transparency obligations for AI-generated content and synthetic media.

## 3. CORPORATE LEGAL PROCEEDINGS & SEC DISCLOSURES (ITEM 3)
- [ ] Disclosure of pending legal actions where damages exceed 10% of current assets.
- [ ] Environmental liabilities and regulatory compliance proceedings.
- [ ] Intellectual property litigation and patent validity challenges.
